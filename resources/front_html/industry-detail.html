Oil & Gas Manufacturing Expertise

IK Saudi Manufacturing has been a trusted fabrication partner for Saudi Arabia's oil & gas sector for over 25 years. We hold Saudi Aramco's Approved Vendor List (AVL) registration and comply with all SAEP, SAES, and SATIP standards, making us a preferred supplier for Aramco, ADNOC, and other GCC national oil companies.

Our Oil & Gas capability spans the entire production chain — from wellhead equipment and flowline assemblies in upstream fields, to gas processing facilities and pipeline systems in midstream operations, to refinery vessel packages and heat exchanger bundles in downstream processing.

Our O&G Product Range

  • ASME VIII Pressure Vessels (Separators, Knock-Out Drums)
  • TEMA Heat Exchangers (Shell & Tube, Air-Cooled)
  • API 650/620 Storage Tanks
  • ASME B31.3 Process Piping Systems
  • Wellhead & Manifold Skids
  • Gas Compression Packages
  • Metering Stations & Filtration Units
  • Structural Steel for Offshore Platforms

Our Journey

IK Saudi Manufacturing
A Legacy of Innovation

Founded in 1999 in Riyadh, IK Saudi Manufacturing began as a specialist steel fabrication workshop serving the local construction industry. Over two and a half decades, we have grown into one of Saudi Arabia's most trusted names in industrial manufacturing.

Today, we operate advanced manufacturing facilities across four cities — Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, and Jubail Industrial City — employing over 2,000 skilled professionals, engineers, and project specialists committed to Saudi Vision 2030.

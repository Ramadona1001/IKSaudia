About This Service

Our Industrial Manufacturing division operates state-of-the-art fabrication shops across Riyadh, Dammam, and Jubail Industrial City. With over 150,000 m² of combined workshop space and advanced CNC machining centers, we deliver precision-grade components and complete assemblies for Saudi Arabia's most demanding industrial applications.

Every product leaves our facility with full material traceability, weld maps, NDT reports, and ASME/API data books. We hold approvals from Saudi Aramco, SABIC, SEC, NEOM, and MAADEN — ensuring your procurement process is seamless.

Key Capabilities

  • Structural steel fabrication up to 5,000 MT/month capacity
  • ASME Section VIII Pressure Vessel manufacturing (Div. 1 & 2)
  • TEMA Class R, C, B Heat Exchanger fabrication
  • Modular skid packages with full E&I integration
  • API 650/620 Storage Tank construction
  • CNC precision machining to ±0.01mm tolerance
  • ASME B31.3/B31.4/B31.8 Piping systems fabrication
  • Full blast, paint, and surface treatment facilities

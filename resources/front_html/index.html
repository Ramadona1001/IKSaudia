Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our services, quality standards, and project delivery.

We provide a comprehensive range of industrial manufacturing services including precision machining, structural steel fabrication, pressure vessel manufacturing, piping systems, electrical and instrumentation work, heavy equipment supply, and full EPC (Engineering, Procurement, Construction) project execution.
IK Saudi Manufacturing holds ISO 9001:2015 certification and complies with ASME, ASTM, API, AWS, and NACE international standards. We are an approved vendor for Saudi Aramco and SABIC, and work under strict QA/QC regimes with third-party inspection protocols.
We serve the Oil & Gas, Petrochemicals, Energy & Power, Construction, Industrial Manufacturing, and Infrastructure sectors across Saudi Arabia and the wider GCC. Our expertise spans upstream oilfields to mega-project construction sites.
We use PMI-certified project managers and advanced scheduling tools (Primavera P6) with real-time progress monitoring. Our dedicated planning department performs weekly look-ahead schedules, resource leveling, and critical path analysis to proactively manage delays.

Services & Capabilities

We provide comprehensive industrial manufacturing including structural steel fabrication, pressure vessel manufacturing (ASME Sec. VIII), heat exchangers (TEMA), storage tanks (API 650/620), piping systems, modular skid packages, precision CNC machining, and full EPC project execution. Our facilities operate to Saudi Aramco, SABIC, and SEC approved vendor standards.
Yes. We scale from precision machining of individual components to full EPC (Engineering, Procurement & Construction) projects valued at hundreds of millions of Saudi Riyals. Our project management capability covers all scales — from single equipment items to complete industrial plant construction.
Yes. We offer multi-year Operation & Maintenance (O&M) contracts including planned preventive maintenance (PPM), corrective maintenance, plant shutdowns, turnarounds, and 24/7 emergency response. Our O&M teams are stationed across Saudi Arabia for rapid deployment.

Quality & Standards

IK Saudi Manufacturing holds: ISO 9001:2015 (QMS), ISO 14001:2015 (Environmental), ISO 45001:2018 (Safety), ASME U/U2/S/PP Stamps, AWS D1.1/D1.5 Structural Welding, API Q1 (Quality Program), and is registered on Saudi Aramco's Approved Vendor List (AVL) and SABIC's Approved Supplier List (ASL).
Our ASNT Level II/III certified inspectors perform: Ultrasonic Testing (UT), Radiographic Testing (RT), Magnetic Particle Testing (MT), Liquid Penetrant Testing (PT), Visual Inspection (VT), and Positive Material Identification (PMI). We also facilitate third-party inspection by Bureau Veritas, Lloyd's, SGS, and ARAMCO-appointed TPI agencies.

Projects & Delivery

We use Primavera P6 for scheduling with weekly look-ahead plans, critical path method (CPM) analysis, and earned value management (EVM). Our dedicated Planning & Controls team produces monthly progress reports aligned with Saudi Aramco SAEP-17 and international PMI standards. Our on-time delivery rate is 96%.
Lead times vary by scope and complexity. Small precision components: 2–6 weeks. Pressure vessels and heat exchangers: 8–20 weeks. Structural steel packages: 6–16 weeks. Full modular skid systems: 16–36 weeks. We can accommodate expedited schedules for urgent requirements with premium services.
Yes. While Saudi Arabia is our primary market, we regularly export manufactured equipment to UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Egypt, and beyond. We handle full export documentation, SABER/SASO compliance, customs clearance support, and international shipping coordination.
